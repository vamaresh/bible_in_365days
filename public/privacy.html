Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Data We Collect

Bible Reading Challenge stores your display name, reading progress, reminder time, language, theme, reading plan settings, reading circle information, optional reflections, and optional encouragement reactions.

How We Use Data

We use this data to show your progress, power community leaderboards, send reminders, support reading circles, and let you share encouragement with other readers.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase for app data and OneSignal for push notifications. These services may process technical information needed to provide hosting, database, cloud functions, and notifications.

Community Visibility

Your display name, progress, streak, reading circle activity, reflections, and encouragement reactions may be visible to other users in the app. Do not post sensitive personal information in reflections.

Deletion

You can delete your account and progress from Profile & Settings in the app. This removes your user record and reading progress from the app database.

Contact

For support or privacy requests, use the support contact provided by the app owner.
